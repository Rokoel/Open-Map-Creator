    Layer outer border texture

    Pencil textures follow the active layer’s exterior contour. They update after each stroke, stay out of enclosed interiors, and keep their graphite color under lighting.

    Each block has straight, parallel strokes that meet neighboring blocks. Strokes per block controls internal density; block density controls how many blocks fill the border.

    Upload edge + corner patterns

    Use transparent PNGs or SVGs. Match the edge strip’s height to the square corner’s size.

    1. Edge: horizontal strip, dungeon along the bottom, shading above. Make the left and right ends repeat seamlessly.
    2. Corner: square for an outside top-left turn. The dungeon corner touches the image’s bottom-right point. Match its right and bottom edges to the strip.
    3. Upload both images. The editor repeats and rotates edges, rotates outside corners, and mirrors inside corners. Border width controls their size.

    Print-ready PDF

    The map is tiled across printable areas at your chosen physical cell size.

    Margins on every sheet (mm)

    Margins reserve blank paper on each sheet. The map is split into more pages as needed, keeping every pixel at the chosen print scale. Trim the margins to join the sheets.

    Sheet margins vs. print-app margins

    These margins change the page split before the PDF is created. Larger margins put less map on each sheet and can add pages, while keeping cell size unchanged. Map-wide padding adds space only around the complete map.

    A print app receives already-split PDF pages. Its margin or Fit to page settings may shrink the map or clip its edges; they do not update the page breaks saved in this PDF. Set sheet margins here to suit your printer, then print at Actual size / 100% without adding another scaling or margin adjustment. Trim the blank edges to join sheets.

    Print at Actual size / 100%. Turn off Fit to page or Shrink to fit.
